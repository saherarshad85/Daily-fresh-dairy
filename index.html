Our Story

A Family Farm, Rooted in Care.

Daily Fresh Dairy is a family-owned farm located in Ganjianwali Kalan, P.O. Khas, Ugoki, Sialkot, Pakistan. We are dedicated to bringing you the purest, most wholesome dairy products — straight from our pastures to your table.

Our approach is simple: happy, healthy cows cared for with love produce the finest milk. We believe in transparency, quality, and the timeless values of honest farming.

Our Beginning

Rooted in Sialkot.

Daily Fresh Dairy is a family-owned and operated farm based in Ganjianwali Kalan, P.O. Khas, Ugoki, Sialkot, Pakistan. Our journey began with a simple, honest belief: that real dairy — made with care, from healthy animals, in clean conditions — is one of life's simplest pleasures.

Every day, we work to bring that belief to your table. From our pastures to your kitchen, we're committed to transparency, quality, and the timeless values of honest farming.

Our Promise

Care in Every Drop.

We believe that great dairy starts with great care. Our animals are looked after with attention and respect, and our processes are designed to preserve the natural goodness of every product we make.

From milking to packaging to delivery, we focus on doing the simple things well. No shortcuts, no compromises — just honest work and quality you can trust.
